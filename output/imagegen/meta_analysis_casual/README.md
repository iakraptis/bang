# Casual-player meta infographic series

Seven portrait social cards derived from `docs/meta_analysis.md`, reframed for board-game players without AI-training terminology. Every card is labeled as a simulated four-character meta snapshot.

1. Team edge
2. Characters who beat their team average
3. Matchup swing
4. Strong pairs versus combo chasing
5. Defensive table habits
6. Most-picked legal cards
7. Meta in 30 seconds

Artwork is original AI-generated illustration, not official game art.
