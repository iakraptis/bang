# Prompt set

Built-in image generation was used with shared direction: portrait 4:5 social-media infographic; casual Wild West board-game audience; one large idea per post; mobile-readable text; dark saloon tabletop; parchment and brass; blue Law / rust Outlaw accents; original character and item card illustrations; no copied commercial card art, logos, or watermarks.

The seven prompts covered: team win-rate edge; side-adjusted character standouts; best/worst Law matchup; three strong pairs with small synergy values; defend/attack/targeting/table tempo; legal-option card pick rates; and a six-tile meta summary. Technical AI/PPO terms were excluded, while a simulation-scope footer was retained.

Final correction prompt: replace only `CARDS PLAYERS REACH FOR` with `CARDS THIS META REACHES FOR` to avoid implying human-observation data.
